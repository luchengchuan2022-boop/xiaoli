# Cross-Scale Contract

Each level has its own job:
- ATOM: one perceptual/dramatic delivery unit.
- EPISODE: local movement and state consequence.
- SEQUENCE: escalation, turn, carry-over.
- ARC: meaningful transformation.
- SEASON: major thematic/relational/world movement.
- SERIES: cumulative promise and identity.
- IP: durable creative DNA and future space.

A lower-level beat may not silently redefine a higher-level invariant. A higher-level plan may constrain a lower level but cannot fabricate evidence of an event that has not happened.

Every planned future must declare `HORIZON`, `DEPENDENCIES`, `OPTION_VALUE`, `REVERSIBILITY`, `COST`, and `RISK`.
