# Objective Vector

Creative quality is multi-objective, not one scalar score.

Represent evaluation as dimensions such as:
- character_truth
- dramatic_value
- emotional_clarity
- cinematic_value
- continuity_safety
- world_consistency
- audience_comprehension
- novelty
- production_feasibility
- model_feasibility
- longform_future_value

Hard constraints are gates. Soft objectives are optimization dimensions.

A high score cannot compensate for a hard-gate failure.
