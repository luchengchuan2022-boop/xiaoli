# Reveal Contract

Every major reveal declares: `SETUP / AUDIENCE_PRIOR / INFORMATION_ASYMMETRY / REVEAL_METHOD / FAIRNESS / SURPRISE / RETROSPECTIVE_COHERENCE / CONSEQUENCE / PAYOFF_STATUS`.

A reveal is invalid when its surprise depends on information the work could not reasonably support, unless intentional mystery rules explicitly justify the concealment.

Do not confuse character ignorance with audience ignorance.
