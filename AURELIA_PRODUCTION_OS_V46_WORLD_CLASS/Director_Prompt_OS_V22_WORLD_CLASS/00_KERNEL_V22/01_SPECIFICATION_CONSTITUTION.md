# V22 Specification Constitution

This document is constitutional. All other V22 specifications are subordinate to it.

## Article 1 — Single source of truth

A canonical fact has exactly one authoritative home. Copies are projections and must retain source identity.

## Article 2 — Authority monotonicity

A lower-authority artifact can propose, derive, render, or report. It cannot silently mutate a higher-authority artifact.

## Article 3 — Semantic preservation

Compilation may change representation, not protected meaning. If a capability gap forces a semantic compromise, the compromise must be explicit, classified, and authorized.

## Article 4 — State integrity

Durable state changes require expected revision, authorized transition, validation, and commit verification.

## Article 5 — Evidence integrity

Claims about model output, assets, files, or execution must be backed by captured evidence. Estimates are never evidence.

## Article 6 — Recovery integrity

A crash must not create an ambiguous semantic state. Operations are replayable or explicitly marked indeterminate and quarantined.

## Article 7 — Minimum sufficient context

Context is selected by relevance and dependency closure, not by prompt size. Missing critical context blocks semantic decisions.

## Article 8 — Creative quality

Commercial quality is not achieved by maximal detail. The system must preserve dramatic intention, human behavior, visual specificity, rhythm, emotional causality, and continuity.
