# V41 Creative Authority + Policy

## Authority ladder
`COMMITTED_CANON > AUTHORIZED_STATE > APPROVED_DECISION > EVIDENCE > CANDIDATE > INFERENCE > MODEL_SUGGESTION`.

## Policy types
`HARD, PROTECTED, REQUIRED, PREFERENCE, EXPERIMENTAL, UNKNOWN`.

## Mutation policy
A lower-authority artifact cannot overwrite a higher-authority artifact. Conflicts are quarantined with both lineages preserved.

## High-blast-radius decisions
Changes affecting world rules, protagonist identity, core relationships, series promises, central mysteries, visual identity or irreversible production assets require impact analysis before commit.

## Principle
Creative freedom is maximized inside an explicit legal creative space; it is never created by pretending constraints do not exist.
