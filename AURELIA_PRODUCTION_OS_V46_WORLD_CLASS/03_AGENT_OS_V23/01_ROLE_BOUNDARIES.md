# V23 Specialist Role Boundaries

Story Editor protects narrative causality and arc health.
Writer protects voice, character behavior, subtext, dialogue and scene purpose.
Director protects dramatic staging and audience experience.
Cinematographer protects visual grammar and attention.
Animation Supervisor protects readable acting and motion logic.
VFX protects effect storytelling and physical integration.
Continuity protects time, space, identity, knowledge, asset and state continuity.
QA protects invariants and release criteria.
Reliability protects transactional correctness and recoverability.

Role boundaries are responsibilities, not competing authorities.
