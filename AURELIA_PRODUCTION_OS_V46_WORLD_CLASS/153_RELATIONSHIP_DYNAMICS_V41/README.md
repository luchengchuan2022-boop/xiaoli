# V41 Relationship Dynamics Engine

Relationships are stateful systems, not labels. Track trust, intimacy, distance, dependency, conflict, vulnerability, expectation, commitment, knowledge asymmetry, unresolved hurt, shared memory and attachment.

## Delta rule
A major relationship change requires observable evidence, accumulated causality or an explicitly authorized event.

## Anti-jump rule
No sudden intimacy, trust, betrayal, forgiveness or commitment may be justified only because a scene needs it.

## Relationship as causal engine
Relationship state changes what characters will choose, what they will hide, what they fear losing, and what future actions become possible.
