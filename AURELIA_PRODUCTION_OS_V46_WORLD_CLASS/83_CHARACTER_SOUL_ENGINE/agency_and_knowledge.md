# Agency + Knowledge Firewall V35

Before any character choice ask:
1. What does the character know NOW?
2. What do they believe, and what is false?
3. What do they want NOW?
4. What are they afraid of losing?
5. What pressure makes action necessary?
6. What alternatives were available?
7. Why does this choice beat the alternatives for THIS person?
8. What consequence changes them?

No character may use information they have not legitimately acquired.
