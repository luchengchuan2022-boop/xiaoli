# Semantic Contract V35

An object is valid only when:
1. its authority is resolvable;
2. its source meaning is traceable;
3. its temporal scope is explicit;
4. its dependencies are closed or explicitly unresolved;
5. its proposed delta has preconditions, postconditions and impact set;
6. its confidence class is explicit;
7. its semantic hash is reproducible.

TEXT MAY VARY; MEANING MAY NOT SILENTLY VARY.
