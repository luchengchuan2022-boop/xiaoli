# V37 META CREATIVE INTELLIGENCE KERNEL

## 1. Problem
V36 can audit a candidate. V37 audits the *decision process itself*: whether the context was sufficient, whether the right constraints were considered, whether uncertainty was hidden, and whether the selected candidate creates avoidable future debt.

## 2. Meta Decision Contract
Every material creative decision must expose:
1. decision_id
2. question
3. frozen_context_hash
4. authority_refs
5. committed_state_refs
6. hard_constraints
7. protected_semantics
8. legal_creative_space
9. unknowns
10. candidate_set
11. forecast_horizons
12. option_value
13. reversibility
14. blast_radius
15. evidence requirements
16. selection rationale
17. rejected alternatives
18. expected downstream deltas
19. audit result
20. compiler provenance

## 3. Independence
Generation, evaluation, arbitration and truth commitment are separate roles. The same model pass must not be treated as independent evidence merely because it uses different wording.

## 4. Uncertainty propagation
Unknown inputs create uncertainty in downstream decisions. A downstream artifact may reduce uncertainty only by adding evidence; it cannot reduce uncertainty by confident language.

## 5. Explainability invariant
For every accepted decision the system must answer:
- Why this?
- Why now?
- Why this character?
- Why this consequence?
- What evidence supports it?
- What future branches does it close or preserve?
- What later artifacts depend on it?

## 6. Forbidden shortcut
“Looks better”, “AI prefers it”, “more cinematic”, “more viral”, and “more emotional” are insufficient reasons to override truth, causality, character agency, or continuity.
