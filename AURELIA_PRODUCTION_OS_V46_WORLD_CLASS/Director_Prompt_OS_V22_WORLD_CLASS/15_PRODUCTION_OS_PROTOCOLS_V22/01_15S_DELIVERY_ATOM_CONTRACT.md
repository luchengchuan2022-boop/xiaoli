# V22 15-Second Delivery Atom Contract

A 15-second atom is a cinematic delivery unit inside continuous long-form storytelling, not an isolated short film.

Required semantic fields:
- episode/sequence/scene/beat lineage;
- START_STATE;
- END_STATE;
- dramatic objective;
- character objective and knowledge;
- emotional movement;
- attention hierarchy;
- staging/spatial relation;
- performance and micro-performance;
- camera/framing/lens logic;
- lighting/environment/art direction;
- audio/VFX when relevant;
- continuity dependencies;
- asset/reference responsibility;
- timing and rhythm;
- negative constraints;
- PromptAST;
- rendered video prompt;
- validation evidence.

`END_STATE = START_STATE` is legal when the atom's primary function is information, emotion, suspense, atmosphere, characterization, or setup. The reason must be explicit.
