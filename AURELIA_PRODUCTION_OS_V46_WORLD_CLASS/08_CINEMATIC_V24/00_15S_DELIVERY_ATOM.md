# V24 15-Second Delivery Atom Contract

Required: `atom_id, duration, source_beat, start_state, end_state, dramatic_purpose, attention_plan, shot_plan, performance, staging, camera, lighting, environment, audio, vfx, assets, continuity, constraints, prompt_ast, evidence_requirements`.

The atom may contain one or multiple shots. Every shot has a temporal interval. Total intervals must cover the delivery duration without unexplained gaps or overlaps.
