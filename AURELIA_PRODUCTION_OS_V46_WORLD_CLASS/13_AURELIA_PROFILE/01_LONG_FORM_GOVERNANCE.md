# AURELIA LONG-FORM GOVERNANCE

For 1600–8000+ episodes:
- preserve established Canon
- preserve committed State
- preserve character knowledge
- preserve relationship trajectory
- track foreshadow/payoff
- minimize new lore
- diagnose continuity before invention
- require explicit change classification for retcon or canon change

AURELIA creativity operates inside these constraints; constraints are not a request to flatten the story.
