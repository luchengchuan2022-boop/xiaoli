# Story Graph V21

The story graph is causal, not merely chronological. Nodes represent events, choices, revelations, consequences, relationship transitions, and emotional turns. Edges record causality, dependency, knowledge flow, and payoff obligations.

Every episode slice must declare:
- inherited active threads;
- local dramatic objective;
- irreversible or reversible changes;
- new obligations created;
- unresolved threads preserved;
- payoff candidates and deadlines.

A short episode cannot reset the graph to manufacture an isolated hook.
